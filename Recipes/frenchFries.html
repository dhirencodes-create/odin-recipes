French Fries

Description

French fries are a popular side dish made from potatoes that are cut into strips and deep-fried until crispy.

Ingredients

  • potatoes
  • oil for frying
  • salt

Steps

  1. Cut potatoes into fry-shaped strips.
  2. Heat oil in a deep pan or fryer to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Fry the potato strips in batches until golden brown and crispy.
  4. Remove from oil and drain on paper towels.
  5. Sprinkle with salt and serve hot.
