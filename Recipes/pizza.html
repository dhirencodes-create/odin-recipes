Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a flatbread base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings.

Ingredients

  • flour
  • water
  • yeast
  • olive oil
  • tomato sauce
  • cheese
  • various toppings (e.g., pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers)

Steps

  1. Mix flour, water, and yeast to make the dough.
  2. Roll out the dough and place it on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Top with cheese and desired toppings.
  5. Bake in the oven until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted.
