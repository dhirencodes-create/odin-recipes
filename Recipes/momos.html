Momos

Description

Momos are a type of dumpling that is popular in Nepal and India. They are typically filled with meat or vegetables and are steamed or fried.

Ingredients

  • maida
  • water
  • salt
  • ginger-garlic paste
  • chopped vegetables

Steps

  1. Mix maida, water, and salt to make the dough.
  2. Roll out the dough and cut it into small circles.
  3. Place a spoonful of filling in the center of each circle.
  4. Fold the dough over the filling and pinch the edges to seal.
  5. Steam or fry the momos until cooked.
